Projects

Four pieces of work, start to finish

SQL · MySQL — Spring 2026

Operations Performance & KPI Analysis

Built a relational MySQL database over an orders dataset and wrote the queries behind fulfilment rate, SLA compliance, cancellation rate and average order value. Used CTEs, window functions (RANK, LAG) and rolling averages to surface demand trends, peak-hour patterns and operational gaps — then turned those trends into service-performance findings.

  • MySQL
  • CTEs
  • Window functions
  • KPI design

MS Excel — Spring 2026

Sales Performance & Revenue Optimization Dashboard

Analysed 500+ e-commerce transactions to trace revenue across months, categories and products. Built an interactive dashboard on Pivot Tables, XLOOKUP and INDEX-MATCH, identified the seasonal patterns and category-level drivers behind a revenue decline, and shipped it fully automated — no hardcoded cells, so the report refreshes on new data.

  • Pivot Tables
  • XLOOKUP
  • INDEX-MATCH
  • Dashboarding

Campaign strategy — 2026 · Independent

MARS Cosmetics — “College Starter Kit”

A full go-to-market campaign for the Gen-Z college segment (17–23). Framed the barrier as three things — budget, decision fatigue, purchase hassle — and answered it with a bundled 5-in-1 kit and the line “Stop buying makeup like it's homework.” Structured a four-phase rollout (teaser → drop → campus tour → UGC contest) across social, campus pop-ups and micro-influencers, and priced the bundle at ~35% savings versus à la carte to carry the AOV argument.

  • Positioning
  • Bundling strategy
  • Channel planning
  • Gen-Z research

Creative strategy — 2026 · Assignment

Haldiram's — “Thanda Funda”

Concept and naming for a beverage line aimed at exam-stressed students aged 15–25, built on the insight that the only real break in a study day is the walk to the water cooler. Designed the activation mechanics — bottle-code quizzes, campus leaderboards, Smart Sip booths and an Instagram #SmartSipChallenge — then stress-tested the concepts against a memorability rubric (emotional insight, minimal dialogue, visual storytelling, clear payoff).

  • Concept development
  • Consumer insight
  • Activation design
  • UGC mechanics
